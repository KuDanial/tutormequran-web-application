Support Hub

How Can We Help You?

Search our knowledge base or browse frequently asked questions below for fast resolutions to common student and tutor queries.

For Students

How do I know the Quran tutors are qualified?

Every tutor on TutorMeQuran must upload their official Quranic Ijazah certificate (verification credentials) during registration (FR8). Our admin team manually audits each certificate to verify authenticity (FR10) before the tutor can list their classes.

How do I book a lesson with a tutor?

First, browse tutors on the Browse page and select a suitable teacher. You can click "View Profile & Book" to view their full credentials and calendar availability. The system displays their real-time calendar and lets you book a paid slot. Our overlap blocker ensures you cannot book a slot that is already booked by another student.

Can I edit or reschedule a booking?

Yes. Both students and tutors can request modifications to a booked class (FR6) up to 24 hours before the session. Go to your Dashboard, view your active booking, click "Reschedule", and select an alternative vacant slot on the tutor's calendar.

How do online classes take place?

To keep classes affordable and flexible, TutorMeQuran excludes built-in video streaming. Instead, online classes are hosted via external channels like Google Meet, Zoom, or WhatsApp Video. Once a booking is confirmed, a meeting link will be shared in your dashboard.

For Tutors

How long does the Ijazah verification process take?

Our administrative team reviews uploaded Ijazah certificates within 24 to 48 hours of registration. You will receive an email and system notification once approved. Tutors with approved certificates are visible in search listings instantly.

How do I set my class scheduling availability?

Log into your Tutor portal and go to "Schedule Availability". You can select repeating time blocks (weekly availability) or select specific dates. Students will only be able to book sessions within these vacant intervals.

How do I receive payments for my tutoring?

Students pay safely through the system upon booking confirmation. Once you conduct a session and mark it as completed, and the student approves, the funds are accumulated and disbursed to your registered Malaysian bank account.

What if a student cancels at the last minute?

Cancellations within 24 hours of the scheduled start time may incur a fee, depending on the cancellation reason. The student's fee is partially or fully transferred to the tutor to compensate for the slot block.
