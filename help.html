How can we help you?

Find answers about learning, tutor applications and DuitNow payments.

Open FAQ Chat
How do I book a tutor?

Browse the public tutor directory, open a verified profile and select an available slot. Review the hourly rate, class mode and lesson time before confirming your booking.

How do I upload my DuitNow receipt?

Open My Bookings, select your booking and choose Upload Payment. Check the recipient and amount, transfer directly to the tutor, then attach your receipt. The tutor acknowledges receipt of funds and the admin records the final payment status.

How long does tutor verification take?

The admin reviews your certificate after you apply. No review time is guaranteed in this prototype. Check your application status for the decision and any correction requested.

When can I set my hourly rate?

After your certificate is verified, open your teaching profile to add pricing, qualifications, biography and availability. Those teaching tools remain locked while your application is pending or rejected.

What do Draft, Private and Public mean?

Draft keeps an unfinished profile unlisted. Private keeps a completed profile unlisted and pauses new bookings. Public makes a verified, completed profile discoverable and bookable. Existing bookings remain accessible when a listing is made private.

Can students and tutors edit or cancel bookings?

Both can open a booking and choose Edit or Cancel. Coordinate changes with the other person. Cancelling a booking does not automatically refund a DuitNow transfer; payment issues require admin review.

What if the tutor has not received my payment?

The tutor can report a mismatch instead of confirming receipt. The payment stays pending for admin review. Check the original transaction before making another transfer.

Who can use TutorMeQuran?

Students and independent Quran tutors aged 18 and above who reside in Malaysia. Lessons focus on Quran-related subjects.

Where do online lessons happen?

Students and tutors agree on an external platform for their lesson. TutorMeQuran handles booking and payment records and does not provide built-in video calls.

Need help with a specific booking?

Include your booking ID when explaining a payment or scheduling issue.
