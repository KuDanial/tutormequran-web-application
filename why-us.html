Our Value Proposition

Why Choose TutorMeQuran?

A trusted, transparent, and structured system built specifically to elevate the quality of Quranic home education and online tutoring in Malaysia.

1. Academic Trust: Ijazah Verification

Traditional tuition directories allow anyone to call themselves an Ustaz or Ustazah. TutorMeQuran resolves this through mandatory credential audits. Tutors must upload their official Quranic Ijazah (recitation certification) upon registration (FR8). Our admin team conducts a manual review against authorized standards before approving profiles (FR10).

Satisfies Requirement FR8 & FR10

2. Reliable Bookings: Overlap Blocker

Students can view their chosen tutor's live availability calendar and book a specific slot in real-time. The database system contains block validation triggers which automatically block double-bookings, preventing scheduling conflicts between tutors and students.

Satisfies Requirement FR5 & NFR3

3. Secure Cashless Payments

Pay securely for your booked sessions through integrated Malaysian payment gateways. The platform automatically tracks completed slots, holds funds securely, releases payments to tutors after verification, and generates printable invoices and receipts for accounting.

System Scope Integrated feature

4. Personalized Portals

Three distinct portals ensure users only see what is relevant:

  • Students: Book classes, manage schedules, track payments, write ratings.
  • Tutors: Edit bio rates, upload certificates, accept/reject booking invites.
  • Admins: Manage accounts, audit certifications, compile weekly business metrics.
Access Control & User Roles (Section 3.2.3)

5. Booking Management

Needs change, and TutorMeQuran adapts. Both students and tutors can request booking modifications (date/time changes) directly in their portals (FR6), cancel slots within grace periods, and view a complete history of previous and current lessons (FR7 & FR9).

Satisfies Requirement FR6, FR7, & FR9

6. Admin Auditing & Reporting

Administrators have full administrative authority (FR11) to coordinate tutors and students. The admin panel features analytical dashboards to monitor user registration rates (FR12) and compile printable performance or billing reports (FR13) for auditing purposes.

Satisfies Requirement FR11, FR12, & FR13
