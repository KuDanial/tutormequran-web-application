FAQ

Quick answers about your TutorMeQuran account.

How do I find and book a tutor?

Browse tutors, check their hourly rate and teaching mode, then choose an available slot. Find a tutor.

Where are my pending and past bookings?

Open My Bookings under Management in your profile menu. Pending bookings contains upcoming lessons and bookings still in progress. Past bookings contains completed or cancelled lessons.

How do online and physical lessons work?

Agree on the meeting link or venue with your tutor through the booking conversation. Online lessons use an external platform such as Google Meet or WhatsApp.

When and how do I pay?

In this booking flow, pay after the class is complete. Open the payment step to see your tutor’s DuitNow QR, pay using your banking app, and upload the successful transaction receipt.

Who verifies my receipt?

Your tutor confirms that the funds were received, or reports a payment issue. Admin reviews the receipt and records the final verification.

Can I edit or cancel a booking?

Open the booking to edit or cancel it and coordinate changes with your tutor. Use the Help Centre if you need assistance.

How do I ask about a refund or an attendance issue?

Submit a request, choose Billing or refund or Attendance issue, and explain what happened. You can add a photo. A request does not automatically approve or process a refund.

Where can I edit my profile?

Open My Profile, then choose Edit profile. You can update your details and profile picture.
