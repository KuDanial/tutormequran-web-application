Cancellation policy

  • If Student cancels early, booking status becomes Cancelled by Student.
  • If Tutor cancels early, booking status becomes Cancelled by Tutor.
  • If cancellation happens on the booking day, it should be marked as Late Cancellation.
  • Admin can review dispute cases if payment issue happens.

Early means before the booking date; the day before is early. On the booking date (Malaysia time), record Late Cancellation and who cancelled. No automatic refund or cancellation fee is applied by this interface.
